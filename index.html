AI Text Cleaner

Paste text from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. The typographic characters these models add are swapped for plain keyboard characters as you type. The words are not changed, and nothing leaves the browser.

Why remove these characters

The em dash, curly quotes and the ellipsis character are not new. Typesetters have used them for centuries, and they appear on almost every page of printed novels, newspapers and essays. Chat assistants now produce them constantly, the em dash above all, and many readers have come to see them as a sign that a text was written by a machine. One em dash in an email, a LinkedIn post or a cover letter can make a reader trust the rest a little less, even when a person wrote every word.

Replacing them with the plain characters on a keyboard removes that distraction, so the text is judged on what it says. It also fixes practical problems: curly quotes break code and command lines, and invisible characters cause odd line breaks, failed searches and mismatches in forms and spreadsheets. The wording itself is not changed, so this alone does not make AI-written text read as human.

What gets replaced

  • Curly quotes and apostrophes become straight " and '.
  • An em dash becomes a spaced hyphen ( - ); an en dash becomes a hyphen.
  • The single ellipsis character becomes three periods.
  • Non-breaking spaces (including the narrow one, U+202F) become normal spaces.
  • Zero-width spaces, joiners, word joiners, byte order marks and soft hyphens are removed.

Limitations

Every replacement is always on. This page does not strip Markdown (headings, bold, bullets), emoji, arrows or other symbols, and it cannot switch individual replacements off or choose what an em dash becomes. It also replaces non-breaking spaces that a language like French uses on purpose.
