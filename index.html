AI Text Cleaner

A small, basic tool. Paste text from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and it swaps the fancy characters those bots love for the normal ones on your keyboard. Your words stay the same, and nothing gets sent anywhere: it all runs in your browser.

What it does

  • Curly quotes and apostrophes become straight " and '.
  • Em dashes become a hyphen with spaces ( - ), and en dashes become a hyphen.
  • The ellipsis character becomes three dots.
  • Weird spaces become normal spaces, and invisible characters get deleted.

That's it. It doesn't touch Markdown or emoji, and there are no settings.

Why I made it

Em dashes and curly quotes used to be just good typography. Every printed book is full of them. Then chatbots started putting them everywhere, and now a lot of people see an em dash and think "a bot wrote this". I got tired of fixing them by hand in emails and posts, so I made this to do it for me. It also helps when you paste text into code or a form, where curly quotes and invisible characters break things.

It only changes characters, not words. If the text sounds like a bot, it will still sound like a bot.

Want more options, like removing Markdown and emoji or choosing what each character turns into? There's a fuller AI text cleaner on srchtool.com, plus a bunch of other free text and developer tools.
